# The Warm Hallway

A tiny Node/TypeScript program for the make.fun bounty "One Line of Earned Pride". It writes one quiet, earned-pride line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), after validating that the line is a single non-empty line of 1 to 240 characters.

Entry line:

> At 6 a.m. the old caretaker finally got the school boiler to hold its heat, and he stood in the warm hallway a minute longer than he needed to, just listening.

Run it with: `node main.ts`
